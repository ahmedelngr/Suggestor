# Role: Team lead (Ahmed)

## Mission
Keep five people unblocked and pointed at the same goal.

## Owns
Decisions, reviews, integration, and the club's technical document.

## Responsibilities
- Resolve [open decisions](../open-decisions.md) by the date they block someone; record outcomes in [decision-log.md](../decision-log.md).
- Review PRs promptly (aim: within 48 hours) and enforce the guardrails.
- Run a weekly check-in: what shipped, what is blocked, what is next.
- Protect scope: say no, or defer, to out-of-scope requests.
- Keep the club document (still describing the old events plan and 7 people) up to date.

## Guardrails
- Decide in the open (decision log), not in private chats.
- Do not become the bottleneck: delegate reviews per folder via CODEOWNERS.
